Game-by-game AI review workflow

Every game receives a source-linked fact review. An AI reviewer can add supporting arguments, a counterargument, what is priced in and what would change the decision. AI prose cannot override a failed audit or change a probability.

The server worker requires a model API key and model configured privately on the host. The existing Codex research publisher can also process the review queue. Paid model requests default to zero.

No paid AI requests are made unless a key, model and positive budget are configured.

Model research & audit policy

Spread and moneyline probabilities share a margin model. The totals replacement failed retrospective release checks. The joint-score simulator is a separate challenger and cannot promote picks. More simulations do not prove accuracy.

Every evaluated quote is checked independently for game identity, freshness, signs, sources, probability math, known personnel risks and model version. A paper candidate must also clear the provisional 3% conservative-return screen and current coverage checks. This threshold does not validate profitability.
