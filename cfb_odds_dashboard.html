Field Guide

Executive Summary

Barnum & Payne runs an independent power-rating model alongside the live sportsbook line for every game, purely as a second opinion — it is not a pick generator, and nothing is labeled a proven edge until backtesting says so.

The spread model (Elo-based) has been tested five independent ways across three full seasons — a fundamentals refit, naive line-movement following, success/havoc-rate features, key-number crossing, and cross-book dispersion. All five came back null. Five methods clearing 50% by chance roughly a quarter of the time, and getting a clean sweep of nulls instead, is stronger evidence than one null test would be. It's shown on every card only as a transparent reference, never flagged or logged as more than no proven edge.

The totals model (PPA-based) found something more interesting but not yet trustworthy: 53.6% against a held-out season it was never fit on (n=513, 95% CI 49.3–57.9%). A follow-up check on whether the market's own later action corroborates that read came back unstable across seasons — doesn't overturn the result, doesn't support it either. Stays unconfirmed until a second clean season decides it.

Three real bugs were found and fixed along the way (a totals sign error, and team-name mismatches that substituted the wrong school's data for three games). One real, unfixable limitation remains: the model increasingly under-predicts the market's own number as the market's margin gets larger. A non-linear correction was tested and found no effect — real blowouts likely get capped by backups and garbage time in a way a two-number rating structurally can't see.

Model Confidence keeps a large disagreement from ever being mistaken for a strong signal — it scores how much to trust the inputs, completely separate from how big the gap is. Every disagreement gets frozen into a backtest log the moment it's seen, graded against the real result and CFBD's own closing line once the game is played.

Model Spread (Elo) No proven edge 5 independent methods, 2023–2025 — 47.4–52.6% ATS, all straddling the 52.4% breakeven line.
Model Total (PPA-based) Unconfirmed 53.6% on held-out 2025 (n=513, 95% CI 49.3–57.9%). Market's own closing-line movement doesn't corroborate it.
Model Total (SP+ preseason) Untested No per-week SP+ history exists to backtest against without leaking future data into the past. Sanity-checked only.
Model Confidence Rule-based, not backtested Deducts for known data gaps. Reliable by construction, not yet proven predictive on its own.
Opportunity Score Not built Deliberately deferred — weights won't be hand-assigned until real backtest volume exists.
Glossary
Core Model
Model Disagreement
The gap between the model's projection and the current sportsbook line. Never called an "edge" or "signal" — those are reserved for something backtesting has actually confirmed.
Model Spread No proven edge
Elo differential converted to margin, plus home-field and rest adjustments. Reference only — never used to flag or sort.
Model Total Unconfirmed
League-average baseline adjusted by each team's offensive/defensive PPA, scaled by a fitted pace factor.
Elo Rating
CFBD's pregame power rating. Used for the spread model because it's correct-as-of-kickoff by construction, unlike SP+.
PPA
Predicted Points Added — CFBD's per-play efficiency metric. Positive offensive PPA is good; positive defensive PPA means the defense allows more than average.
SP+
CFBD's opponent-adjusted overall rating, split into offense/defense. Used as a preseason fallback before any PPA exists.
Pace Factor
The constant converting combined PPA into points. Refit via OLS on training data only, after a guessed value produced nonsense totals.
Baseline Total
The league's season-average combined score — the totals model's starting point before any team-specific adjustment.
MODEL_FLAG_THRESHOLD
The disagreement size (currently 3 pts) above which a totals game gets flagged/badged. Spread is never flagged, at any size.
Confidence & Reliability
Model Confidence
A 0–100 score for how much to trust a game's inputs — never for how big its disagreement is. Docks points for preseason, weeks 0–3, and missing PPA/Elo data.
Low Model Confidence banner
Appears below 50/100, naming the specific reasons the score was docked.
Data reliability vs. predictive power
Two deliberately separate questions: can the inputs be trusted (confidence) vs. has this number actually beaten the market historically (the backtest).
Market Signal Engine
Market Activity
The card block showing opening vs. current line, plus movement over 1H/6H/24H — built from this app's own recorded snapshots.
Line History / Snapshot
Every open/refresh saves a timestamped snapshot, persisted on-device so movement survives closing the tab.
Movement window (1H/6H/24H)
The nearest snapshot at or before that many hours ago. Reads N/A honestly if nothing that old exists yet.
N/A (as used here)
Means "not enough history yet" — never a stand-in for zero or "no movement."
Steam move / Reverse line movement Not built
Needs timestamped multi-book history and ticket/handle data this app doesn't collect today.
Backtesting & Grading
Recommendation Log
Every Model Disagreement, frozen the first time it's seen, so it can be graded later without lookahead bias.
Frozen
A logged recommendation's original fields are never rewritten. Only the outcome gets filled in afterward, by grading.
Grading
Matching a frozen entry to its completed game and checking whether the model's favored side won against the line available when it was logged — not the closing line.
CLV (Closing Line Value)
Whether the closing line moved toward the model's side after logging. Positive = the market later agreed; negative = it didn't.
ATS / OU
Against The Spread / Over-Under — shorthand for "did the favorite cover" and "did the total go over," used in every hit-rate figure.
Push
The result landed exactly on the line — excluded from hit-rate math since it's not a decision either way.
95% Confidence Interval
The range a hit rate could plausibly fall in given the sample size, so a small-sample percentage doesn't get mistaken for a real result.
Train / Test Split
Fitting a constant only on past seasons, then checking it on a season the fit never saw — keeps a number from being quietly reverse-engineered to look good.
Lookahead Bias
Letting information from after a game (or season) leak into a projection that's supposed to be as-of-kickoff. The reason Elo replaced SP+ for the spread backtest.
Opportunity Score Not built
A future composite of disagreement, confidence, market signal, and price quality. Left unweighted until real graded volume exists to fit it empirically.
